### Chapter 1
##### The Wreck Picker
I met a strange old wreck picker by the name of Basilio. Though I should be careful in trusting him, he has offered to buy any scrap I find on the beach. I can find him in an old farm house north of where I landed on Idra.
##### An Abandoned Farm
I came across a small abandoned farm near the beach where I came ashore on Idra. It's pretty derelict but it might serve well enough as a makeshift camp.
- derelict: Derelict buildings or places are not cared for and are in bad condition
- makeshift: temporary and of low quality, but used because of a sudden need
##### A Foul cave
I discovered a cave near the spot where I landed on Idra. A foul stench of rotting flesh and seaweed lies thick inside.
##### Astronomer's Journal
I found a strange jounal that seems to have belonged to an Imperial astronomer. It appears the poor man felt the stars are somehow 'wrong' after spotting a strange star-cluster in the southeastern quadrant.
 - quadrant: a quarter of a circle
##### The Telescope
Though I cannot really put into words what I saw, I used the astronomer's telescope and I saw terrible things. I am beset by a deep certainty that something is gravely wrong on this island.
- beset: having a lot of trouble with something, or having to deal with a lot of something that causes problems
- gravely: seriously
##### Surviving Sailors
I came across a group of survivors from the Zephyr. They were holed up in some abandoned fishing huts and their leader, Snow, asked that I send any other survivors from the Zephyr to their camp.
- hole up: to stay in a safe place, often as a way of avoiding something or hiding from someone
##### Monolith
There are strange monoliths on Idra. Though they appear to be ancient, it seems someone is still performing rituals at them.
##### The zephyr
Apparently the wreck of the Zephyr has shifted enough in the tides that it is now possible to enter it.
##### The second keeper
The lighthouse in Idra seems to have had a second keeper: A man named Caspar who seemingly went mad dreaming about caves underneath the lighthouse. His writing mentions a blade calling him there.
##### The light House
I made it to the lighthouse and met its keeper Mathias: A haggard old fellow that had barricaded himself on the top floor of the structure. To his credit, he's managed to keep the lighthouse running throughout the calamities that are befalling this islkland. No doubt this is critical to the survival of those still caught out at sea.
- haggard: looking ill or tired, often with dark skin under the eyes
- To someone's credit: used to say that someone should be praised for something, although there may be other things about them that are not good: 
- calamity: a serious accident or bad event causing damage or suffering
##### Smuggler's cache
I found an abandoned smuggler's cache in one of the caves on Idra. Perhaps there are more like it to be found on the Outer Isles?
- cache: a hidden store of things, or the place where they are kept
##### A ritual dagger
I recovered a strange dagger of indeterminate age from underwater caves beneath the lighthouse on Idra. It appears to have been a form of ritual implement and I suspect it might still hold both significance and power.
- indeterminate: not measured, counted, or clearly known
##### Driina the sepulchite
I was joined by a Sepulchite cleric by the name Driina. She intends to search for a way into the city of Horryn which has apparently been overrun by cultists. Before we leace the camp, she insist we make a stop by the camp's de-factgo leader: Someone named Markus who is holed up in a stable nearby.
- overrun: spread over or occupy (a place) in large numbers (범람하다, 점령하다.)
- make a stop: to go to a place for a visit or errand during a journey
- de-facto: actual
##### Markus the chamberlain
I met and spoke with the de-facto leader of the refugee camp north of Horryn: Apparently the town has been overrun by crazed cultists. Worse, the city-gates are apparently sealed making it impossible to get back in. For now my energy might be well spent trying to ease the plight of the refugees.
- plight: an unfortunate, difficult, or precarious situation
##### The weathermaiden's plight
A crewman from the ship "Weathermaiden" has asked that I seek out the first mate Zhara. They are holed up in a large sea-cave at position 66 by 91 on the Outer Isles map. That should make it north-west of the refugee-camp.
##### Jakup is done talking
Jakup, the Idra goat farmer, has made it clear he has nothing more to say regarding the issue of his hidden goats.
##### The hidden glen
I learned the way to a hidden glen near the refugee camp on Idrak. The best way to get there is to find and follow the goat tracks behind the old goat enclosure on Jakup's farm.
- glen: a deep, narrow valley, especially among mountains
##### The gravedigger
I met a poor fellow outside the Idran refugee camp. He had the thankless task of burying dead refugees. I offered to help him before the horrid entities that roam the island made short work of him.
- horrid: Extremely unpleasant, disagreeable, or offensive; causing horror or disgust  
- entity: Something that exists independently, has a distinct identity, or possesses a particular set of characteristics; a thing with distinct and independent existence  
- make short work of: 빠르게 처리하다, 쉽게 없애다.  
##### Pilgrim's shirines
I found an old pilgrim's shrine. If I could somehow learn the proper prayers and rituals I could probably complete the pilgrimage myself I wanted.
##### A feast for lizards
I found scraps of rotting flesh in the lizard-cave on Idra. I wonder if the reptiles would gorge themselves if they had more flesh?
- gorge: To eat a large amount of food greedily  
##### Embla in Horryn
Frantzo the former innkeeper just told me that Embla stayed at his inn Horryn in the days leading up to the calamities that saw the city overrun.
- leading up to: A phrase that describes the period or events that gradually progress towards a significant moment or outcome (로 이어지는)

### Chapter 2
##### The waif
I met a young girl in the ruins of Horryn and managed to win her trust with some food. She has proven quite resourceful and has successfully managed to evade the Reavers. It turn out she had quite a bit of information about them. She's currently hiding in an alley west of the plaza.
- waif: a child or animal without a home or enough food and care, usually thin and dirty in appearance  
##### The art dealer
An art-dealer down in the underhalls beneath Horryn is looking to buy strange and unusual artifacts if I find any.
##### Main gate opened
The main gate of Horryn is now opened!
##### Oleg's right to rule
I met one of the three Reaver chieftains: Oleg. He's the oldest of the three and claims to hold 'sacred' tablets that gives him right to tule over his siblings; Kala and the Butcher. He's asked me to seek them out and remind them of the fact.
##### The Butcher's designs
I met the Reaver chieftain that calls himself The Butcher. He's determined to win the favor of the one the Reavers call Father by subjugating his siblings: Oleg and Kala. For starters he's asked me to steal a pair of unholy tablets that are currently in the possesion of Oleg. They are currently in the possession of Oleg. They are being Kept in tunnels under Oleg's lodge in the east end of town.
- subjugate: to defeat people or a country and rule them in a way that allows them no freedom  
- lodge: a small house in the country, used especially by people on holiday or taking part in sports, or one on land belonging to a large house  
##### Sea-gate open
The Horryn sea-gate is now opened!
##### Reaver cellar
A pair of Reavers appeared to be guarding something in the cellar under Oleg's lodge.
##### The Reaver Tablets
I gave the Reaver Tablets to the Butcher. He now intends to make an offering of Oleg as part of his ascent to Father's side. He's asked me to go tell Kala he wants her at his side for the final slaughter.
- offering: something that you give or offer to someone  
##### Oleg's plan
I told Oleg that the Butcher has gotten hold of his unholy tablets. The news caused the old chieftain to decide to attempt to end the Butcher. I suspect that Oleg doesn't stand a chance against him alone so perhaps I should ask the third cheiftain, Kala, to side with Oleg? Together they might be able to take down the Butcher.
- get hold of: to find someone or obtain something  
- stand a chance: to have a chance of success  
##### Oleg the Gray is dead
The Reaver chief Oleg the Gray is dead.
##### Queen Kala is dead
The Reaver chief queen Kala is dead.
#####  The Butcher is dead
The Reaver chief known as the Butcher is dead.
### Chapter 3
##### A strange growth
I came across a strange fungus growing on some of the fresh corpses in the crypt under the Horryn temple. It's difficult to say what it is but is seems to be out of place there.
- crypt: a room under the floor of a church where bodies are buried  
### Chapter 4
##### Horryn is saved
I managed to storm the keep in Horryn and stamp out the last remnants of the Reavers. Though hundreds are dead and the city in ruins, Horryn appears to be safe for now.
- stamp something out: to get rid of something that is wrong or harmful
##### Iago rejoins his Crew
Iago has gone ahead to rejoin his crew and begin preparations for us to sail for Gradla. He awaits us up in a large sea-cave at position 66 by 91 on the Outer Isles map. That should makeoit north-west of Horryn.
- make it: to reach a particular place, goal, etc.
##### The maiden sets sail
We've taken to the high seas on the Weathermaien. We must, however, stay close to shore lest we invoke the wrath of the behemoth that stalks the deeper seas of the Outer Isles.
- lest: for fear that — used to say that you do not want something to happen
##### Colony collapse
I found several dead or dying bee-hives in Firgol. It seems the bees there are fighting amongst each other.
##### Frigol bees
Despite assurances that 'Firgol bees do not sting', I found the bees in one of the hives to be quite aggressive. I may need to find some way of pacifying them if I am going to get the honey.
##### The ascension chamber
I found the chamber where Calipero's apprentices ho to ascend as a reward for their service. Grotesquely, the chamber appears to be nothing more than a crematorium: a furnace where a corpse can be burned and reduced to ashes  
##### Trapped mine tunnel
I've rigged one of the tunnels in the mine at Adler's Helm to collapse. The trap can be triggered by using a rope to pull away the remaining supprot-beam and I'm certain it could be done rapidly if need be.
rig: arrange the outcome of by means of deceit; "rig an election"
### Chapter 5
##### The false tomb
I found and opened the sarcophagus in the priory catacombs central tomb-chamber. Surprisingly, it turns out to be empty! A decoy perhaps?
- decoy: a person or thing that attracts people's attention so they will not notice someone or something else
##### The first sarophagus
I found a sarcophagus under the Priory with the following description.
"Carroleth of the Crystal Eye - First Grandmaster of the Order of the Crystal Eye."
##### The third Sarcophagus
I found a sarcophagus under the Priory with the following description.
"Kalamar the wise - Third Grandmaster of the Order of the Crystal Eye."
##### The second Sarcophagus
I found a sarcophagus under the Priory with the following description.
"Rothrax the Gray - Second Grandmaster of the Order of the Cristal Eye."
##### The Gong
I performed a ritual using the gong outside the tomb chamber. I have a strong feeling it was correctly performed though I cannot tell exactly to what purpose. Perhaps some kind of trap was deactivated?
##### Tunnels in the Dark
I found a secret exit in the tomb-chamber that leads to tunnels engulfed by magical darkness. I feel certain the way forwards lie through these tunnels though I must take care not to get lost in the dark as I attempt to navigate them.
