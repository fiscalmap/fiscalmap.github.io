First, let's understand that "The Tower of Fools" (original Polish title: "Narrenturm") is the first book in Sapkowski's Hussite Trilogy, set in 15th century Silesia (now parts of Poland, Czech Republic, and Germany). The book contains many Polish, Czech, and German names.

## Main Polish Pronunciation Rules

Polish pronunciation is quite consistent once you learn the rules. Here are the key points:

1. 'cz' is pronounced like 'ch' in "church" (한국어로는 '치')
2. 'sz' is pronounced like 'sh' in "ship" (한국어로는 '시')
3. 'rz' and 'ż' are pronounced like 's' in "measure" (한국어로는 '즈')
4. 'ł' is pronounced like 'w' in "water" (한국어로는 '우')
5. 'w' is pronounced like 'v' in "very" (한국어로는 '브')
6. 'c' is pronounced like 'ts' in "cats" (한국어로는 '츠')
7. 'j' is pronounced like 'y' in "yes" (한국어로는 '이')
